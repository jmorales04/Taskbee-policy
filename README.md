# Taskbee-policy
TaskBee Policy
1. Introduction

Welcome to TaskBee!

TaskBee is a personal and business task management application designed to help individuals and professionals organize tasks, manage responsibilities, and improve productivity.

At TaskBee, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. Our application is designed with a privacy-first approach, allowing users to manage their tasks without requiring the collection of personal information.

TaskBee does not intentionally collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the application developer.

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskBee handles information and protects user privacy.

2. Information Collection

TaskBee is designed to operate without collecting personal information.

We do not require users to provide:

Full names or personal identification

Email addresses

Phone numbers

Physical addresses

Financial or banking information

Contact lists

Precise geographic locations

Personal identification numbers

Advertising identifiers

TaskBee does not require users to submit personal information to the developer to access its core task management functionality.

3. Task and Application Data

TaskBee allows users to create and manage information related to their personal and professional activities.

This may include:

Tasks and task descriptions

Project information

Task priorities

Due dates and reminders

Task completion status

Organizational preferences

Application settings

This information is used exclusively to provide the application's task management functionality.

TaskBee is designed to keep user-created information under the user's control.

TaskBee does not intentionally collect user-created task information for advertising, marketing, or commercial data analysis.

4. Data Storage

TaskBee is designed to prioritize user privacy and data security.

When task information is stored locally on a user's device:

Information remains within the application's local storage.

TaskBee's developer does not have access to locally stored task information.

Information is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

Users maintain control over the information they enter.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and any locally stored information.

5. Data Sharing

TaskBee does not sell, rent, trade, or intentionally disclose personal information to third parties.

TaskBee does not use user-created task information for:

Targeted advertising

Marketing campaigns

Behavioral profiling

Commercial data brokerage

Unauthorized third-party distribution

Our goal is to provide a productive and private task management experience.

6. Advertising and Tracking

TaskBee is designed without behavioral advertising or user profiling.

TaskBee does not intentionally:

Track users across unrelated applications.

Build advertising profiles.

Monitor browsing activity.

Sell user activity information.

Collect personal information for targeted advertisements.

We believe users should be able to manage their responsibilities without unnecessary tracking.

7. Third-Party Services

TaskBee may be distributed through Google Play, which operates independently under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google Play may process information related to application downloads, purchases, payments, and platform security.

Such information is handled according to Google's own policies and is separate from TaskBee's intended collection practices.

TaskBee does not independently collect payment card information through its core task management functionality.

8. Data Security

Protecting user information is important to TaskBee.

The application is designed to minimize unnecessary access to personal information and limit data handling to what is required for application functionality.

Users are encouraged to:

Keep their devices updated.

Use appropriate device security features.

Protect access to their devices.

Maintain backups when supported.

Although reasonable measures may be implemented to protect application information, no electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

TaskBee does not intentionally maintain a developer-controlled database of users' personal information.

Information stored locally within the application remains on the user's device until it is deleted through available application features or removed through applicable device storage controls.

Users may remove locally stored application information using their device's application management settings.

Uninstalling TaskBee may remove locally stored application information, subject to Android's backup and restoration functionality.

Because TaskBee is not designed to collect personal information on developer-controlled servers, the developer generally does not possess personal information to retrieve or delete.

10. Children's Privacy

TaskBee is a general productivity application intended for personal and professional task management.

TaskBee is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

If future application features require the collection of personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those practices and any applicable protections.

11. User Privacy Rights

TaskBee respects user privacy and supports the principle that individuals should maintain control over their information.

Because TaskBee is designed not to collect personal information, the developer generally does not maintain personal records associated with individual users.

Users retain control over information stored locally on their devices.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

TaskBee may update this Privacy Policy to reflect application improvements, new functionality, legal requirements, or changes in data handling practices.

Any changes will be reflected through an updated effective date.

If future features introduce personal information collection, this Privacy Policy will be revised to accurately disclose the information collected, its purpose, and how it is handled.

13. Our Privacy Commitment

Your Tasks. Your Data. Your Privacy.

TaskBee is built around a simple principle:

Your productivity should never come at the expense of your privacy.

We are committed to providing a reliable, professional, and privacy-conscious task management experience.
